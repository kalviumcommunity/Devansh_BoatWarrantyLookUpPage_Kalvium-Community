## Project

Warranty Lookup & Repair History System for Boat

## Problem We Are Solving

Boat customers need a simple way to check whether their product is still under warranty and view its previous repair history. Currently, warranty information can be difficult to access or verify.

Our system will allow customers to enter their **product serial number** and retrieve:

* Product information
* Warranty expiry date
* Warranty status
* Paginated repair history

The system will also provide **admin users** with the ability to upload warranty PDFs and attach them to the appropriate product records.

## Target Users

### 1. Customers

Customers who own Boat products and want to quickly:

* Verify their warranty status
* Check when their warranty expires
* View previous repair records

### 2. Admin Users

Boat staff/admins who need to:

* Manage product warranty information
* Upload warranty PDFs
* Attach warranty documents to product records
* Maintain accurate product and repair information


Responsibilities may overlap when needed, and the team will review important changes together.

## Definition of Done

A feature is considered complete when:

1. It works according to the agreed requirement.
2. It has been tested.
3. It does not break existing functionality.
4. The code is committed and pushed to the repository.
5. Any necessary documentation has been updated.

## Team Goal

Build a reliable and simple warranty lookup system where a customer can enter a valid Boat product serial number and immediately understand their warranty status and repair history.
